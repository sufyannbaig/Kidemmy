 Kidemmy is a parenting operating system that helps parents raise their kids well, from birth through age 15, without having to piece together advice from a hundred different sources.  Most parenting apps stop at age five and disappear right when things get harder: puberty, screen time, emotional regulation, character and values, and the everyday question of what to actually teach a kid this week. Kidemmy stays with the family through the whole journey, not just the baby years.  Core features:  Multi-kid household support — one parent account manages every child, each with their own age, interests, and development track, instead of forcing families into a single-child model. Daily guidance — age-appropriate activities and learning prompts for each kid, every day, so parents always know what to focus on without researching it themselves. Growth-stage training plans — as each kid grows, their plan evolves with them, covering physical, cognitive, emotional, and social development across the full 0–15 range. Parent School — resources and coaching to help parents build their own parenting literacy, not just track their kid's milestones.  Kidemmy exists because parenting literacy is missing today, and most tools only cover the easy, well-documented years. This is built for the years that actually need help.
